If you've ever tried to buy a ticket to a show in Nigeria, you know this story. The fans who waited all morning lose. The organizer takes the insults. The only winners are the people running bots and fake accounts.

It happens because ticket sites count accounts, not people. One tout with a hundred accounts gets a hundred tries. You get one.

So I built Kovenra 🎟️, a ticket counter that counts people.

Here's how it works:

🧍 One person, one fair seat. For hot shows, every buyer proves once with @selfxyz and their passport that they're a real, unique person. Kovenra never sees the name or the passport, just a yes or no. And if someone sends AI agents to buy for them, all their agents share that one person's limit. No bot army.

💸 Naira for the organizer, in seconds. Fans pay in dollars from their phone over x402 on @Celo. Kovenra converts it to naira (cNGN) on @Textileprotocol and sends it straight to the organizer's own wallet. No waiting days for a payout and no chasing anybody.

🎲 Fair Drop for shows that sell out. Instead of a race the fastest bot wins, verified fans enter a draw, and a Celo block picks the winners. Nobody can rig it, and anyone can check it.

🔁 Resale only at face value. Can't make it? Hand your ticket back and the next fan buys it at the original price. Touts have nothing to gain.

📊 A public fairness report for every event: tickets sold, how many different buyers, every payment and payout, all on-chain.
