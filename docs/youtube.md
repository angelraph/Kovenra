## Title
Kovenra: fair event tickets for real people in Nigeria, paid in naira on Celo

## Description
You know how it goes. A show goes on sale, it's sold out in minutes, and by evening the same tickets are on WhatsApp for three times the price. Bots and touts win, real fans lose.

Kovenra is a ticket counter that counts people, not accounts.

• One person, one fair seat: buyers prove once with the Self app and their passport that they're a real, unique person. Kovenra never sees the name or passport.
• Naira for the organizer in seconds: fans pay in dollars over x402 on Celo, Kovenra converts it to naira (cNGN) on Textile FX and sends it to the organizer's own wallet.
• Fair Drop: for shows that sell out, verified fans enter a draw and a Celo block picks the winners.
• Resale only at face value, so touts have nothing to gain.
• A public fairness report for every event, and a QR ticket that works once at the door.
• Built for AI agents too: Kovenra is ERC-8004 agent #10137 with a verified Self Agent ID (#223).

Already in real use: Elysian Home is selling tickets on Kovenra for Elysian Home Laughter Night in Enugu, with naira paid out on Celo for every sale.
