# How the Kovenra site was designed

Before building the new site, we went through figma.com, mobbin.com and framer.com properly. We looked at every section, the menus, the pricing and FAQ pages, how things move, and how each site looks on a phone. Then we kept the ideas that suit Kovenra and dropped the rest.

Here's what we took from each one, and where it ended up.

## What we liked about each site

**Figma**
- The hero is a row of fanned-out cards. The middle one is full size and the side ones are smaller. It moves on its own, with a pause button wrapped in a little progress ring and a "5 of 8" counter.
- A white prompt box sits on top and types a request while the card behind it shows the result.
- Headlines come in two tones: a dark first sentence, then a grey second one.
- A logo strip slides sideways and has its own pause button.
- Product shots sit on a textured coloured panel, with named cursors moving around.
- Big quotes have a thin line on the left and one large number beside them.
- The final "Get started" is one huge full-width button.
- Small labels and footer headings use a monospace font.

**Mobbin**
- Headlines fade in from a blur.
- Small app icons sit inside the headline and flick from one icon to the next.
- Story sentences stay grey until you scroll to them, then light up.
- A grid of six tiles, each with its own little looping animation.
- An "ask your agent" demo: a chat bubble, a shimmering "working on it" line, then the results slide in.
- Floating icons drift up and down on both sides of a centred headline.
- The page lifts off a dark footer, and a huge cropped wordmark sits right at the bottom.
- The phone menu is a full-screen sheet with big links.
- The FAQ is a set of dark rounded cards that open when you tap them.

**Framer**
- A black page with a big, tight headline on the left.
- The product sits inside a frame with a glowing edge.
- The agent panel shows each step it took and how long it took.
- They show a full working copy of the product, not a flat screenshot.
- One big box split by thin lines into feature cells, each with a small live piece of UI.
- Live metric bars and a "live visitors" chart.
- A terminal window with tabs and a copy button.
- The final call to action is a real text box you can type into, with suggestion chips under it.
- The footer has a little "all services online" status line.

## Where it ended up on Kovenra's landing page

1. **Header.** Blurred black bar, a simple menu with a live card, and a full-screen phone menu.
2. **Hero.** A big headline with icons flicking inside it (ticket, verified, naira, QR).
3. **Hero stage.** A glowing gold frame with fanned-out event photos. A glass card types what a fan wants and ticks off each step: checked with Self, paid, ticket ready. The dollar price uses the live rate.
4. **Built on.** A sliding strip of the tech underneath, with a pause button.
5. **The problem.** A two-tone sentence, then story lines that light up as you scroll.
6. **Live board.** The current naira rate, the latest Celo block, Kovenra's totals and recent activity. All real.
7. **Why it's fair.** Six tiles with photo backgrounds and small looping animations.
8. **Organizer view.** An example dashboard in a window, with a door-staff cursor moving around. It's clearly labelled as an example.
9. **Every kind of night.** Photo cards for concerts, campus shows, gospel nights, comedy, tech meetups, owambe, food festivals and plays, each with a price or rule control on top.
10. **Agents.** A chat demo showing each step, plus a terminal with a copy button for the skill file.
11. **The promise.** Our covenant quote next to a live count of bots turned away.
12. **Naira.** Floating ₦ and ₮ tokens around "Paid in dollars? Received in naira."
13. **FAQ.** The most common questions, with a link to the full page.
14. **Last step.** A real "What are you hosting?" box that opens the create form, then one big gold button.
15. **Footer.** The page lifts away, then link columns, a live Celo block line, and a giant "Kovenra" at the bottom.

## Pages of their own

Events, How it works, For organizers, For fans, For AI agents, Pricing, FAQ, About, Trust & safety, Live proof, Brand, Contact, Terms, Privacy, and a friendly 404.

## Photos

The event photos are free Unsplash photos, with no watermarks. We picked them to show real nights out: crowds, choirs, comedians, dancers, panels and food stalls. Each card has a drawn version underneath, in case a photo doesn't load.

## Look and feel

- **Gold:** `#FDB129`, taken straight from the logo.
- **Backgrounds:** near-black `#0A0A0B` and charcoal `#141416`.
- **Text:** off-white `#F4F4F5`.
- **Verified:** mint green `#34D399`.
- **Fonts:** Plus Jakarta Sans for headlines (it matches the round letters in the logo), Inter Tight for reading, and JetBrains Mono for labels and numbers.
- **Motion:** quick and calm. If someone has reduced motion turned on in their phone settings, the animations stay still.
